Vision, mission & values

Aligned with the EMBRACE message across Stella Education.

Vision

Cocoon celebrates each child’s unique character and believes every child is special. We strive to inspire a lifelong love of learning and nurture global-minded learners who embrace diversity and creativity, respect nature, and pursue excellence in education from the earliest years.

Mission

Cocoon’s mission is to honour diversity and create a nurturing environment where children from all backgrounds grow through a balanced education. We foster creativity, mutual respect, and care for our environment, empowering children to become compassionate, globally minded, independent lifelong learners who make a positive impact on the world around them.

Founders & system

Cocoon is built by a team committed to Stella Education and early years education in Vietnam.

Ms. Duong Thi Kim Thoa

Founder of Cocoon Montessori Preschool and a mother of two daughters in early years and primary. Motherhood deepened her dedication to education. She believes that by creating an ideal school for her own children, she opens the same opportunities for every child.

Her commitment to a nurturing, experience-rich environment reflects both love for family and responsibility to the wider community of young learners — in line with Stella Education.

Dr. Nguyen Thi Thu Huyen

With over 20 years in education, Dr. Huyen has helped shape Vietnam’s education landscape, including Stella Education and its schools. She has held senior roles in public, bilingual, and international schools.

She is passionate about high-quality, inspiring programmes and widening access to excellent education for Vietnamese children — from preschool through later years in one shared value system.

Early years programme

A day at Cocoon

A rhythm designed for preschoolers: safe, warm, and full of room to explore.

Mornings begin with a gentle welcome, then child-chosen work and small groups: Montessori materials, stories, music, outdoor play, and healthy snacks. Lunch and rest follow; afternoons bring creative projects, short inquiries, or nature exploration by age.

Hours: Monday–Friday, 7:30 a.m. – 4:30 p.m. Saturday visits can be arranged.

  • Drop-off & learning day: within 7:30 – 16:30 (details by class)
  • Indoor & outdoor activities through the day
  • Meals & rest follow the age-group schedule (see Age groups)

* Times may change with the school calendar; confirm when you book a tour or speak with admissions.
